Quotes

Existence
"The universe doesn't owe us meaning. It only owes us the chance to go looking for it."

~ Overheard, source unverifiable

Nature
"Every predator is just a hypothesis about the prey's attention, tested at full speed."

~ Written on a napkin, author unknown

Science
"A model is a lie that tells the truth often enough to be useful."

~ Some statistician, probably

Mind
"Consciousness might just be the universe's way of double-checking its own work."

~ 3am thought, credibility uncertain

Orbits
"Precession is just the universe admitting that nothing spins in isolation."

~ Definitely not Kepler

Poetry
"A good metaphor is a bridge you don't notice you're crossing until you're already on the other side."

~ A poem I half-remember, half-invented

Curiosity
"Ask the question that embarrasses you a little. Those are usually the ones worth asking."

~ Advice I gave myself, later forgot the source of

~ Cold comfort, but comfort nonetheless
